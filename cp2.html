CP2 Discourse Community & Vocabulary

About CP2

In CP1, you chose a topic, an artifact, and a first research question. Now you are going to find the discourse community already talking about your area and learn some of the specialized language that can help you investigate it more precisely.

Choose Your Path

You have two ways to complete CP2. Choose one and read that page before beginning:

Path 1: Guided AI
Use AI to help identify your discourse community and generate possible vocabulary, then independently verify the terms it suggests.

Path 2: Non-AI Research
Find your discourse community and its vocabulary yourself by searching and reading the places where that community actually communicates.

Both paths lead to the same CP2 and are worth the same number of points. The documentation you provide is slightly different because the terms come from different places.

Before You Start

Have you had second thoughts about the topic or artifact you chose in CP1?

If so, now is the time to change it.

You are still early enough in the Culminating Project that changing direction is manageable. Once we move beyond CP2 into primary research and published research, changing your entire project becomes much harder.

If you change your topic, artifact, or both, simply identify the new direction at the beginning of CP2.

What Everyone Submits

Regardless of which path you choose, your CP2 must include:

  • One paragraph identifying your discourse community and explaining how it connects to your topic, artifact, and developing inquiry.
  • 10–15 useful vocabulary terms from that discourse community, each defined in your own words and documented according to the requirements of your chosen path.
  • An updated research question at the end.

Your updated question may use some of the vocabulary you learned if those terms actually help you ask the question more precisely. Do not force specialized language into the question just to prove you found it.
