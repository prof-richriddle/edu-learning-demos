About CP2

In CP1, you chose a topic, an artifact, and a first research question. Now you are going to find the discourse community already talking about your area and learn some of the specialized language that can help you investigate it more precisely.

Choose Your Path

Path 1: Guided AI Use AI to help identify your discourse community and generate possible vocabulary, then independently verify the terms it suggests. Path 2: Non-AI Research Find your discourse community and its vocabulary yourself by searching and reading the places where that community actually communicates.

Both paths lead to the same CP2 and are worth the same number of points. The documentation you provide is slightly different because the terms come from different places.
